# fr-common-words.txt

The 50,000 most frequent French words (one per line, counts removed), used by the experimental proper-noun
correction as « everyday words » that are never replaced on their own.

Source: [hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), `content/2018/fr/fr_50k.txt`,
built from OpenSubtitles 2018. Licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
(Hermit Dave). Modified: counts removed.
